*Every warband cleared for play in Mordheim Australis 2027, in one place. Warbands are graded by source quality — Grade 1A are official GW/Fanatic lists, Grade 1B are unofficial lists released through GW/Fanatic channels, and Grade 1C are community lists vetted and approved for this campaign.*

| Warband | Style |
|---|---|
| [Cult of the Possessed](grade-1a/cult-of-the-possessed) | Chaos cult — monstrous Possessed backed by expendable brethren |

| [Mercenaries (Middenheim)](grade-1a/mercenaries-middenheim) | Melee-leaning humans — strong champions, wolf-cloaked brawlers |

| [Skaven](grade-1a/skaven) | Fast, numerous and cheap — sneaky Eshin weapons, expendable vermin |
| [Undead](grade-1a/undead) | Vampire-led horde — free zombies, ghouls and dire wolves |
